## Why a separate real-data edition?

The earlier `Project 03_Trading Strategies Lab` uses a seeded, simulated 500-day series to teach mechanics offline. **This lab supersedes that data approach for strategy evaluation:** per the assignment requirements, every price, return, and performance figure here must come from genuine historical data downloaded from Yahoo Finance. Simulated regimes are replaced by *observed* regimes in 2015–2024 history (2015–19 bull, 2020 COVID crash/rebound, 2022 bear, 2023–24 bull), analysed in Module 8. Do not copy results, data, or expected-return tables from the simulated lab into this one — they describe different (invented) prices.

## Key correctness rules enforced throughout

1. `Position = Signal.shift(1)` — a close-formed signal can never earn that same bar's return.
2. Donchian channels and z-scores use only past data (shifted / rolling / train-only) — never full-sample statistics.
3. Costs are charged on **position changes**, in decimal (bps ÷ 10⁴), and net results are reported next to the benchmark under identical assumptions.
4. Daily bars: next-Open fills (M6) vs lagged close-to-close (M5) are *different stated approximations*; intraday stop/target ordering is unknowable from OHLC and is never invented.
5. `Close` excludes dividends, so buy-and-hold understates total return — stated as a limitation, with an `Adj Close` check as a stretch, not hidden.
